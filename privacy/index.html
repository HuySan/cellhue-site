Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what the Cellhue add-on for Google Sheets ("Cellhue", "we", "us") can access and what happens to that information. In short: Cellhue works only inside the spreadsheet you use it in, has no server of its own, and does not send your spreadsheet data anywhere. The only outside request is a check of your Pro license key with Polar.

1. What Cellhue can access

When you install Cellhue, Google asks you to allow these permissions (OAuth scopes):

ScopeWhy Cellhue needs it
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly To read cell values and colors, and to write formulas, in the spreadsheet where you use Cellhue. It gives no access to your other spreadsheets or files.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui To show the Cellhue menu, sidebar and dialogs inside Google Sheets.

2. Spreadsheet data

  • Cellhue works only inside the current spreadsheet.
  • Cellhue has no server of its own. The add-on code that runs in Google makes no external requests.
  • Your spreadsheet data does not leave Google and is not transferred to us or to anyone else.

3. Pro license key check

Cellhue Pro license keys are issued by Polar. When you activate a key, and each time you open the Cellhue sidebar after that, the sidebar sends the license key, an activation ID and a short activation label to Polar's license API (api.polar.sh) directly from your browser, to check that the key is valid. No spreadsheet data and no data from Google APIs is sent.

Cellhue saves the key, its activation ID, the purchaser email that Polar returns for the key and the time of the last check in your own Google account's user properties for the add-on (Apps Script UserProperties). This information is not sent to us.

4. Usage statistics

Cellhue keeps simple usage counters locally, in your own Google account's user properties for the add-on (Apps Script UserProperties). These counters are never sent anywhere.

5. Payments

Cellhue Pro is sold through Polar, which acts as our merchant of record. Payment details are entered on Polar's checkout and handled by Polar. We do not see your payment card details. Polar's own privacy policy applies to the information you give them.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your data. Cellhue does not transfer data from Google APIs to anyone.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

Cellhue's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Cellhue uses data from Google APIs only to provide and improve the features you see in the add-on.
  • Cellhue does not transfer this data to others, does not use it for advertising, and does not sell it.
  • No human reads this data.
  • Cellhue does not use this data to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

8. How we protect your data

  • Data stays inside Google. Cellhue runs as Google Apps Script in Google's infrastructure. Spreadsheet data is read only while a formula, the sidebar or a menu action runs, is held in memory for that action, and is not copied to any database, file or server of ours. We have no server and no database.
  • Least access. Cellhue asks only for the two scopes listed above. spreadsheets.currentonly limits access to the one spreadsheet where you use Cellhue.
  • Encrypted in transit. The only outside request, the Pro license check, goes over HTTPS (TLS) directly from your browser to Polar. It contains no spreadsheet data and no data from Google APIs.
  • Protected storage. The few items Cellhue stores (see section 9) are kept by Google in Apps Script properties. User properties are private to your Google account and are available only to the Cellhue code running for you. We cannot read them.
  • No human access. Because we never receive your Google user data, nobody at Cellhue can read it. Source code changes are made only by the developer, and no secrets or user data are kept in the public code.

9. Data retention and deletion

We do not retain Google user data. We have no copy of your spreadsheet data, and we cannot access what Cellhue stores in your account. What exists, where it lives and how to remove it:

DataWhere it is storedHow long it is kept and how to delete it
Spreadsheet values and colors Your spreadsheet only Not stored by Cellhue. They are read in memory while an action runs and are gone when it finishes. Your data stays in your file until you delete it.
Cellhue formulas, a hidden helper sheet named _cellhue and a named range CELLHUE_REFRESH Your spreadsheet Kept in the spreadsheet until you delete them. Delete the formulas, the _cellhue sheet (Right-click its tab, or View → Hidden sheets) and the named range (Data → Named ranges) at any time. Cellhue recreates the helper sheet only if you use it again.
Formula separator setting for the spreadsheet language Spreadsheet's document properties Kept with the spreadsheet. It is deleted when you delete the spreadsheet.
Pro license key, activation ID, purchaser email returned by Polar, time of last check Your Google account's Apps Script user properties for Cellhue Kept while the Pro key is valid. They are removed automatically the next time you open the sidebar after the key is refunded, revoked or deactivated, for example when you remove the activation in your Polar customer portal. You can also stop Cellhue from using them by uninstalling Cellhue or removing its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
Local usage counters Your Google account's Apps Script user properties for Cellhue Kept in your account, never sent to us. Removed with the Cellhue data above.

To ask us to delete anything related to you, or to ask what we hold, email idiosgamecorporation@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. Since we hold no Google user data, we will tell you so and help you remove the items above. Payment and order records belong to Polar, which keeps them under its own policy and legal obligations.
