Cellhue vs Custom Count and Sum

Updated November 9, 2026

Both are Google Sheets add-ons that sum, count and average cells by color. Custom Count and Sum is the larger of the two, with many more functions. Cellhue is smaller and simpler, with a one-time price and narrow permissions. This page compares them so you can pick the one that fits.

Cellhue is not affiliated with Base:10. "Custom Count and Sum" is the name of their product. Their details below come from their public Google Workspace Marketplace listing, checked on November 9, 2026, and may have changed since.

Choose Custom Count and Sum if

  • You need to total across several sheets in one formula.
  • You need to filter or total by font style (bold, italic, underline, strikethrough), or you need max, min or median by color.
  • You want a team plan for a whole domain.

Choose Cellhue if

  • You mostly need sum, count and average by color in one sheet, and you want the formulas to look like SUM and COUNTIF.
  • You prefer to pay once ($19) or stay on a free plan without a trial clock.
  • You want an add-on that is limited to the spreadsheet you open it in and does not ask for your Google account email.
  • You want to see every color in a range with its totals before writing a formula.

The same limit applies to both

Google Sheets does not recalculate a formula when only a cell color changes, so any color add-on can show an old total until it refreshes. Cellhue refreshes when you open the file and on Extensions → Cellhue → Refresh all, and Pro refreshes by itself while the sidebar is open. Neither tool reads colors from conditional formatting rules.

Common questions

Is Cellhue a Custom Count and Sum alternative?

For sum, count and average by fill or font color in one sheet, yes. It does not cover cross-sheet totals, font styles or max, min and median.

Can I switch without rewriting my sheet?

Function names and arguments differ between add-ons, so formulas have to be rewritten. Cellhue's sidebar inserts the formula for a color you click, which makes this quick for small sheets.

Can I use both?

Yes. Add-ons are installed separately, and their functions do not conflict by name.
